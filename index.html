About Me

Hello! I'm Brandon Sariwati, a digital marketing professional specializing in paid search advertising. This portfolio showcases my knowledge and expertise in PPC marketing.

What I Do

I've spent the past five years working in paid search, managing multi-million-dollar budgets to capture high-intent users through Google Ads and Microsoft Ads. Most of my experience comes from regulated environments, where precision, compliance, and strong execution really matter. I focus on building and optimizing scalable search programs across Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, and SA360, using GA4 to understand what's actually driving results.

My Expertise

  • Paid search execution across SA360, Google Ads, and Microsoft Ads
  • Pharma search strategy within regulated, compliance-driven environments
  • Campaign structure, keyword strategy, and negative keyword governance
  • Bid optimization, budget pacing, and automation at scale
  • Ad copy testing and asset iteration through approval workflows
  • GA4-based conversion tracking, analysis, and optimization
