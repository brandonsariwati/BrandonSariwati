About Me

Hello! I'm Brandon Sariwati, a digital marketing professional specializing in paid search advertising. This portfolio showcases my knowledge and expertise in PPC marketing.

What I Do

I've spent the past five years working in paid search, managing multi-million-dollar budgets to capture high-intent users through Google Ads and Microsoft Ads. Most of my experience comes from regulated environments, where precision, compliance, and strong execution really matter. I focus on building and optimizing scalable search programs across Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, and SA360, using GA4 to understand what's actually driving results. Along the way, I've improved efficiency and conversion performance while building clean account structures, negative keyword frameworks, and QA processes that make accounts easier to scale and maintain. I also have direct client-facing experience, turning complex performance data into clear insights and practical strategies.

My Expertise

  • Pharma search strategy within regulated, compliance-driven environments
  • Campaign structure, keyword strategy, and negative keyword governance

  • Ad copy testing and asset iteration through approval workflows
